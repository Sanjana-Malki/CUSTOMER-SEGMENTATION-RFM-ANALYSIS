# Customer Segmentation & RFM Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis** to identify valuable customer groups and support data-driven marketing decisions.

Using Python and Power BI, I cleaned and analyzed retail transaction data, segmented customers into five groups, and developed an interactive dashboard with actionable business recommendations.

## Business Objectives
- Identify high-value and at-risk customers.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Develop targeted customer retention strategies.
- Recommend marketing campaign budget allocation.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy – Data cleaning and preprocessing.
- **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX, and dashboard development.
- **DAX:** RFM calculations, customer scoring, and segmentation.

## Project Workflow
1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, duplicates, and invalid transactions using Python.
2. **Data Modeling:** Created a customer dimension table and established relationships in Power BI.
3. **RFM Analysis:** Calculated Recency, Frequency, and Monetary values for each customer.
4. **Customer Scoring:** Applied percentile-based scoring to assign RFM scores.
5. **Customer Segmentation:** Classified customers into five groups using customized business rules.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Created an interactive three-page Power BI dashboard.

## Key Results
- **779,495** cleaned transaction records.
- **5,881** unique customers.
- **36,975** unique orders.
- **£17.37M** total revenue.
- **£469.91** average order value.

### Customer Segments

| Customer Segment | Customers | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Champions | 1,344 | 22.85% |
| Loyal Customers | 1,706 | 29.01% |
| At Risk | 1,205 | 20.49% |
| Lost Customers | 1,140 | 19.38% |
| Potential Loyalists | 486 | 8.26% |

## Business Recommendations
- **Champions:** Strengthen loyalty through rewards and exclusive offers.
- **Loyal Customers:** Encourage upselling through product recommendations and bundles.
- **Potential Loyalists:** Promote repeat purchases with personalized offers.
- **At Risk:** Launch targeted reactivation campaigns.
- **Lost Customers:** Use low-cost win-back campaigns.

**Proposed Campaign Budget Allocation:**  
At Risk – 30% | Champions – 30% | Loyal Customers – 20% | Potential Loyalists – 15% | Lost Customers – 5%.

*Note: This is a proposed strategic allocation, not historical campaign spending.*

## Project Outcome
Developed an interactive customer analytics solution that transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful customer segments and actionable marketing strategies.
